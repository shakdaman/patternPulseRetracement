| ⚪ **Gray** | (faded) | Mitigated | Filled - skip | No trade |

**Visual Keys:**
- **Solid thick border** (2px) = Active or Inverted (trade these!)
- **Thin faded border** (1px) = Mitigated (skip these)
- **"i" prefix in label** = Inverted/Continuation zone
- **No "i" prefix** = Original/Reversal zone

## 🔍 Pre-Trade Checklist

Before taking any signal, verify:

- [ ] **Trend Alignment**: Signal direction matches HTF trend
- [ ] **Strength Score**: Meets your minimum threshold
- [ ] **Risk:Reward**: At least 2:1 (shown on target line)
- [ ] **Structure**: FVG at key support/resistance level
- [ ] **Volume**: Active trading session with liquidity
- [ ] **Pattern**: Clear candlestick confirmation
- [ ] **Position Size**: Calculated for max 1-2% risk
- [ ] **Market Condition**: Trending (for cont) or ranging (for reversal)

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip #1: FVG Stacking
When multiple FVGs stack on top of each other in same direction = **extremely strong zone**. These rarely fail.

### Tip #2: First Touch Rule
The first time price touches an FVG is usually the highest probability entry. Second touch = reduced odds.

### Tip #3: Inversion Patience
After an FVG inverts (turns yellow), wait for price to pull back to it. Don't chase the break.

### Tip #4: Session FVGs
FVGs that form at session opens (London/NY) are statistically stronger. Prioritize these.

### Tip #5: Trail with New FVGs
As trend continues, new FVGs form. Use these as trailing stop levels to lock in profits.

### Tip #6: Strength Score Mean Reversion
Scores above 85 = overextended. Scores below 20 = snap-back likely. Use opposite bias cautiously.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "I don't see any signals"
- Check display settings (all enabled?)
- Decrease Reversal Min Strength to 50
- Increase Continuation Max Strength to 45
- Decrease Min R:R to 1.5
- Try different timeframe

### "Too many signals cluttering chart"
- Increase Reversal Min Strength to 70+
- Decrease Continuation Max Strength to 30
- Increase Min R:R to 3.0
- Increase FVG Min Size to 1.0+
- Decrease Max FVGs to Display

### "Signals keep failing"
- Are you trading against HTF trend? (Don't)
- Are you taking low-strength FVGs? (Filter better)
- Are you trading in low volume sessions? (Avoid)
- Is market in tight consolidation? (Wait for breakout)
- Are you ignoring confluence? (Add filters)

### "Win rate is below 50%"
- Journal last 20 trades
- Identify patterns in losses
- Likely issues: Counter-trend trades, weak FVGs, no confluence
- Solution: Be more selective (top 25% of signals only)
